# Dunkin' India: no official nutrition data collected (0 rows)

## What was checked
- **https://www.dunkinindia.com/** and **https://dunkinindia.com/** (the official India site) returned **HTTP 502 Bad Gateway** for the homepage, `/menu` and `/nutrition` on 2026-11-13.
- **Jubilant FoodWorks**, the India franchisee:
  - https://www.jubilantfoodworks.com/brands/dunkin-donuts has a brand page with no nutrition content and no links to a PDF.
  - Jubilant's stock-exchange filing of 30 March 2026 says it **will not renew the Dunkin' India franchise, which ends 31 Dec 2026**, and that stores are being closed in phases. Press reports put the count at 27 stores in Dec 2025.
- **dunkindonuts.com** publishes a US nutrition PDF. It is **not** an India source, because India recipes and sizes differ, so we did not use it.
- Scribd and blog copies are third-party and were not used.

## Gap
- We found no official India nutrition source. The site is down and the brand is leaving India by the end of 2026.
- Items with no data: donuts, burgers and sandwiches, coffee in all sizes (the default milk is unknown), shakes and coolers.
- Recommendation: don't spend more effort on this brand. If it is needed, use the in-store FSSAI calorie display while outlets are still open.
